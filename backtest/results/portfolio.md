# Levee backtest: the whole population, a shared reserve, a calm period and carry

Generated by `pnpm backtest` from the committed files in `backtest/data/derived`. The method is in `backtest/README.md`, under "Method: population, shared reserve, calm period and carry", which was committed before any of these results were computed.

**Read this first.**

- Every saved position is a simulation result, under the same assumptions as `summary.md`: the keeper, RPCs, NEAR and the MPC network all work, with a fixed delay D.
- The population is every address with native USDC variable debt on the Aave v3 Pools on Base and Arbitrum, liquidated or not. Liquidated positions in the headline set keep their headline paths. Every other path is rebuilt from portfolio reads, actions and oracle prices; its error is in Validation below.
- A member that was not liquidated in reality counts as surviving, with or without Levee. What Levee changes for it is only how much reserve it draws.
- Hyperliquid is not in these numbers. Its only source here holds liquidation fills, with no account states over time.
- No crash frequency is estimated from two windows. Carry is set against each crash as a break-even.

Unless stated: T 1.20, G 1.50, R 30% of debt at arming, D 60 s, cooldown 60 s.

## Levee against the same USDC repaid upfront

Every member holds a reserve of R × its debt at arming. "Upfront" repays that amount at arming instead, with no keeper. Saved counts liquidated members only, because every other member survived either way. Carry is what holding the reserve idle cost over the period against repaying it, at the period's USDC borrow APR.

The pre-registered rule, like the headline model, takes a landed repay off total debt for good. Upfront counts a repay only up to the USDC debt still on the path, so a borrower that later repaid its USDC itself gets no double credit. The "capped" column gives Levee the same treatment. Where Levee beats upfront, it is that double credit.

## How often triggers fire, and where the reserve goes

Per trigger, at R 30%. "Crossed T" counts members armed above T whose HF later fell below T. Of those: liquidated in the period (needed), HF below 1 on the path but not liquidated (a path error or a position too small to liquidate), and the rest, which recovered on their own (false alarms). "Drawn" is reserve sent by the keeper. "By survivors" is the part sent to members that were not liquidated in reality.

### Break-even against one crash

Bonus avoided in each crash per $1,000 of reserve held across the whole population, against a year of carry at the 2025-01-01 to 2026-11-01 mean rate, weighted by reserve held per chain. "Crashes a year to pay" is how many crashes like this one a year would make the bonus Levee avoided equal the interest an upfront repay would have saved. It sets Levee against a reserve that does nothing. Against an upfront repay of the same size there is no break-even for one position: upfront avoided as much bonus as Levee with its repay capped at the USDC debt left, in every row, and costs no carry. Where uncapped Levee is ahead of upfront, the gap is the double credit described at the top.
