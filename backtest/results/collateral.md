# Levee backtest: repay from collateral

Generated by `pnpm backtest` from the committed files in `backtest/data/derived`. The method is in `backtest/README.md`, under "Method: repay from collateral", which was committed before any collateral-mode code existed or any of these results were computed.

**Read this first.**

- Every saved position is a simulation result, under the assumptions of `summary.md` and `portfolio.md`: the keeper, RPCs, NEAR and the MPC network all work, with a fixed delay D. The helper in `docs/SPEC.md` section 9 is modelled from its spec, and the replayed keeper sends without quoting first, which the built keeper does not (assumption 28).
- Each unwind is priced from a real Uniswap v3 QuoterV2 quote at its landing block, read from an archive node, against AaveOracle in the same block. Between fixed ladder amounts the price is interpolated on a straight line, which overstates the cost where it errs, apart from rounding of a token's smallest unit on sales of a few dollars. Validation below gives the error.
- The swap is assumed to execute at the quoted price. Other traders in the same crash, and other Levee positions selling the same asset in the same block, are not modelled: each unwind sees the pool as it was.
- Collateral mode needs no cash. Its comparison with the same USDC repaid upfront is about protection only: upfront needs that USDC in hand.
- No crash frequency is estimated from two windows.

Unless stated: T 1.20, G 1.50, D 60 s, cooldown 60 s, the cheapest usable fee tier at each unwind, and 600,000 gas per unwind sent. Both caps, 100 and 300 bps, are shown side by side.
